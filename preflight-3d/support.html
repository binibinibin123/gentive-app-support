Which airplane is this?

A generic four-seat, high-wing training airplane, not any specific make or model. Checkpoints and their order are typical for that class of airplane. Always use the checklist in your own airplane's POH/AFM and follow your flight instructor.

How do I move around?

Drag to walk around the airplane and pinch to zoom. In Explore, tap a yellow dot to see what to check there.

Restoring the unlock

Tap Unlock all, then Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple ID you bought it with.
