AIRWORTHY? PREFLIGHT CHECKRIDE · EFFECTIVE NOVEMBER 10, 2026

DATA NOT COLLECTED
ITEM 1

Summary

Airworthy? collects no personal data. There is no account and no sign-up, the app makes no network connections, and it contains no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

ITEM 2

What stays on your device

The app keeps one save file on your device: your sound and haptics settings, your checkride record (the most recent rides with results) and where you stopped in the study walkaround. It is never sent to us or anyone else. You can erase the record from the menu, and deleting the app deletes the file. If you use iCloud or computer backups, iOS may include it in your device backup.

ITEM 3

Instructor codes

An instructor code describes an airplane and its conditions, not you. It is shown on screen and typed in by hand; the app does not upload or receive it.

ITEM 4

Purchases

This version has no in-app purchases. Every feature is free.

ITEM 5

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.
