Privacy notice

Operator: gentive · Contact: eps15920@gmail.com
Effective: September 29, 2026

Recordings and documents stay on your device

ScrollPDF uses system Photos and Files pickers to access items you select. Imported video copies, extracted pages, OCR text, titles, edits and PDFs are processed and stored on your device. Apple Vision performs OCR locally. We do not operate a server that receives these recordings or documents. No app account is required.

Local data remains until you delete it in the app or uninstall. Original media, exported copies and separate backups are not deleted by the app's deletion controls. Files you share go to destinations you choose; device backup and iCloud file providers follow your settings and their own policies.

Advertising and purchases

The free app uses Google Mobile Ads and User Messaging Platform (UMP), including optional rewarded ads. Requests specify non-personalized ads and disable publisher first-party identifiers and publisher personalization. The app does not request App Tracking Transparency permission.

Non-personalized ads still process data. Google SDKs may process IP addresses and approximate location, app/device identifiers, advertising interactions, performance and crash data for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention and diagnostics. We do not attach recordings, document text or precise location to ad requests. Consent forms and Settings privacy options depend on your region and consent state. Pro removes in-app ads.

Apple processes purchases, restores, refunds and transaction verification. The app uses verified purchase status to unlock Pro; we do not receive your payment-card information.

This website and email support

This static website has no analytics, ads, upload form or tracking cookies added by us. GitHub Pages hosts it and may process IP addresses and request information for delivery and security under its privacy policy. Hosting retention is controlled by GitHub; we do not specify a separate retention period for its logs.

If you email support, we receive your email address, message and any attachments through Gmail to respond to your request. We retain correspondence only as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable recordkeeping or dispute obligations. Contact us to request access, correction or deletion. Avoid sending private recordings or sensitive information.

Providers and choices

Apple, Google and GitHub may process data internationally under their own policies. You can manage system permissions in iOS Settings and advertising consent through the app when available. Material changes will be reflected in this notice and its effective date.
