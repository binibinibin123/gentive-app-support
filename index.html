gentive · ScrollPDF

A scrolling recording.
A document to keep.

Turn a slow scrolling screen recording into PDF pages you can review, edit and share. Add searchable text with on-device Pro OCR.

Get a clear capture

  1. Record your screen while scrolling slowly downward. Pause on details you want to keep.
  2. Import the recording from Photos or Files. Keep ScrollPDF open while it processes.
  3. Review marked pages, crop edges, reorder or delete pages, then export a PDF.

Free and Pro

Free conversion processes up to the first 30 seconds or 10 pages. A completed, confirmed rewarded ad unlocks one longer conversion; it does not unlock OCR or image export. Ads may not always be available.

Pro Lifetime is a one-time purchase: longer conversions, on-device OCR for searchable PDFs, page image export and no app ads. This version has no subscription. The App Store shows your localized price. Pro and rewarded conversions have safety limits of 60 minutes and 500 pages.

Your original matters

Fast scrolling, animation or horizontal movement can cause missed or repeated content. Uncertain and reverse-scroll sections are retained for review where detected. OCR and stitching are not guaranteed to preserve every character or frame. Compare important output with the original.
