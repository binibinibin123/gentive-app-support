Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-10-02

Game progress stays on this device. Advertising SDK data and support emails are explained separately below.

1. Operator and contact

Keycap Hop is operated by gentive. Contact eps15920@gmail.com for privacy questions or requests to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of information.

2. Game information on your device

The app stores your best score, daily scores, total steps, run count, selected robot and keyboard theme, achievements, tutorial status, and sound, haptics, reduced motion, and graphics settings. We use these for gameplay and to keep your preferences. They remain in app storage on your device and are not sent to a game server.

The game provides no account, sign-in, server storage, or cross-device sync. Gameplay does not request your name, email, contacts, photos, microphone, or precise location.

Local progress is retained through normal app updates. Deleting the app resets local progress; the operator cannot restore it. Any iOS system backup or restore behavior is controlled by your device settings and is not synchronization provided by this app.

3. Optional rewarded advertising

Versions with advertising use Google AdMob. After a run ends, you may choose to watch a rewarded ad to continue once per run. You may start a new run without an ad. Ads require a connection and may be unavailable.

When the ad SDK is active, it may send or process an IP address and approximate location derived from it, device or app identifiers, ad views and interactions, and diagnostic or performance signals during app use or ad delivery. Local game storage does not mean the advertising SDK collects no data.

Google uses information for ad delivery and measurement, service operation and improvement, and protection against abuse. Depending on the SDK and consent state, information needed for delivery, security, or measurement may still be processed if you do not watch an ad or decline personalized advertising. Consult Google's policies for handling, retention, processing locations, and your rights. Providers may process information through global infrastructure.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data from apps that use its services
  • Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure

4. Privacy choices and Limited Ads

The iOS release build requests Google's Limited Ads. It disables personalized advertising and local-identifier-dependent features such as conversion tracking, frequency capping, and unique reach. The app does not request iOS tracking authorization. Publisher first-party ID and publisher ad personalization are also disabled.

Required privacy messages are presented for your region before the advertising SDK starts. When a privacy-options entry point is required, open the advertising privacy choices in app Settings to revisit your choices. Changing consent does not remove this app's Limited Ads restriction. Limited Ads can still involve information processing for ad delivery, service operation, and fraud or invalid-traffic prevention. Ordinary gameplay and starting a new run remain available when ads cannot be served.

See Google's Limited Ads documentation for the provider's processing model.

5. Support and external services

If you choose to email support, we use your reply address and message to handle your request. Please do not send passwords, authentication information, or unnecessary personal details. We retain correspondence while needed for support and remove information when it is no longer necessary. You may request deletion by email; if retention is legally required, we will explain the applicable scope.

Bundled privacy and support pages can be read offline. Google policy links, your mail client, and advertisements or advertiser destinations may need a connection and are governed by the relevant provider's policies. A hosting provider may process access or security logs when you visit the published web support pages.

6. Deletion and policy changes

Delete the app to remove its local game data. This does not automatically delete information already processed by Google or another provider. Consult their policies and privacy tools for rights and deletion options, or contact the operator.

We update this page when gameplay or information handling changes. Changes affecting your choices will receive any required notice or consent process.
