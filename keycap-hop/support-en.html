How do I play?

Look at the next stair and choose between the forward and turn buttons. A wrong direction or missed timing ends your run. Try the endless mode or the route that changes with the date. Cumulative steps unlock robot colors and keyboard themes.

Where is my progress saved?

Your best score, daily scores, total steps, cosmetics, and settings are kept in app storage on this device. They are retained through normal app updates. Deleting the app resets local progress. There is no account, sign-in, game server, or cross-device sync, so we cannot restore deleted progress or transfer it to another device. iOS Offload App and Delete App may behave differently; use Delete App if you want to remove local data.

Can I play offline?

Ordinary gameplay and these bundled help pages work offline. Rewarded ads, some consent-message operations, email, and external policy links may require a connection.

Why can't I continue with an ad?

A rewarded ad can grant one revival per run, only after the ad SDK confirms the reward. No connection, no available ad, or an incomplete reward condition can prevent a revival. You can start a new run without an ad. If the problem repeats, email your device model, iOS version, app version, and what happened.

Can I change sound and motion?

Toggle sound and haptics in Settings. You can also lower graphics quality or enable reduced motion. If sound is missing, check the in-game sound setting, device volume, and silent mode.

Privacy choices and deletion

If an advertising privacy-options menu is shown, open it in Settings to revisit consent choices. Review iOS tracking permission in device Settings. Delete the app to remove local progress. Read the Privacy Policy for requests about support correspondence or information handled by providers.

What helps us investigate?

  • Device model, iOS version, and app version
  • The screen and steps that led to the problem
  • When it occurred and whether you had a connection
  • A screenshot with personal information removed, if useful

Please do not send passwords or authentication details. We cannot guarantee progress recovery or a reply time, but we will review your report and try to help.
