Privacy policy

Strata has no account and no servers of its own. Your progress stays on your phone. The app shows a Google AdMob banner, and Google may collect device data to serve and measure those ads.

1

What stays on your device

One settings file on your device holds your training progress (best stars and layer clarity for each level), your unit choice (oz or ml) and the fact that you confirmed you are 21 or older. It is never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes it. If you use iCloud or computer backups, iOS may include it in your device backup.

2

Advertising: Google AdMob

A banner from Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) appears on the Train, Layer Lab and Drinks tabs. To serve, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • device identifiers, including the Advertising Identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking;
  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information such as model, OS version and language;
  • ad data such as impressions and taps, and diagnostic data about ad loading.

We do not receive this data. Your pours, progress and age answer are not passed to Google. Google's use is described in the Google Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3

Consent and tracking

Ads start only after the age check. The app then runs Google's User Messaging Platform: where the law requires consent (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), Google's consent message is shown before ads load, and a "Privacy choices" link at the foot of the Drinks tab lets you review it again. Apple's tracking permission prompt may also be shown. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", no IDFA is shared and Google serves non-personalised ads. You can change this any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

4

Age

Strata is about alcoholic drinks and is meant only for adults of legal drinking age (21 or older in the United States). It asks you to confirm your age on first launch and keeps the answer on your device only. Ad requests are limited to content rated for mature audiences.

5

Purchases and children

There are no in-app purchases. The app is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under the legal drinking age.
