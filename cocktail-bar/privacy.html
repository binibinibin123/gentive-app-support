Cocktail Bar 3D

Privacy Policy

Data we collect

Cocktail Bar 3D does not collect, store on a server or share any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Data on your device

Your saved recipes, the bottles you tick in My Bar, your unit choice (oz or ml) and your age confirmation are stored only on your device. Deleting the app removes them.

Age

The app is about alcoholic drinks and is meant only for adults of legal drinking age (21 or older in the United States). It asks you to confirm your age on first launch and keeps that answer on your device only.

Children

The app is not directed to children and collects no data from anyone.
