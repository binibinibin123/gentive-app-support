Cocktail Bar 3D

Privacy Policy

Summary

Cocktail Bar 3D has no account and does not run its own servers. The app shows a banner ad from Google AdMob on its list screens. Google AdMob may collect device data to show and measure ads, as described below.

Data on your device

Your saved recipes, the bottles you tick in My Bar, your unit choice (oz or ml) and your age confirmation are stored only on your device and never sent to us. Deleting the app removes them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Banner ads are provided by Google AdMob. To serve, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect: device identifiers, including the Advertising Identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking; IP address and approximate location derived from it; device and app information such as model, OS version and language; and ad interactions such as impressions and taps. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the IDFA is not shared and Google shows non-personalized ads. You can change this any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Google's use of this data is described in its policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Consent

Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent through Google's consent message before personalized ads are shown. You can review your choices again from the same message.

Age

The app is about alcoholic drinks and is meant only for adults of legal drinking age (21 or older in the United States). It asks you to confirm your age on first launch and keeps that answer on your device only. Ads are limited to content suitable for adults.

Children

The app is not directed to children and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under the legal drinking age.
