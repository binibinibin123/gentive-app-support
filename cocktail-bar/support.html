Strata is a practice glass for layered shots. You pour into a 3D glass, the specific gravity of each liquid decides whether the layer holds, and after every pour the app tells you which interface broke and what stream would have held it.

Why did my layer go cloudy?

The stream was too strong for the gravity gap between the two liquids. Slow the stream, pour over the back of the bar spoon instead of straight from the bottle, or lower the spoon toward the drink. The result screen shows the rate you used against the rate that holds, and Replay shows the moment it broke.

Where do the gravity numbers come from?

Brand-by-brand values from the goodcocktails.com liqueur chart, plus sugar and alcohol tables. How much the spoon and its height calm the stream is the developer's own simple model, not a lab measurement.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), a "Privacy choices" link appears at the foot of the Drinks tab and reopens Google's consent message. Tracking permission can be changed any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Does the ad cover the pour?

No. The banner shows only on the Train, Layer Lab and Drinks tabs and is hidden while you pour.

Who can use Strata?

Adults of legal drinking age only: 21 or older in the United States. The app asks once on first launch. Please drink responsibly and never drink and drive.
