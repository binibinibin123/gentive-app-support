How do I rotate the glass?

On a recipe, drag anywhere on the glass to turn it and tilt the view.

Can I use milliliters?

Yes. On the Cocktails screen tap oz / ml at the top right. Ounces are shown in quarter-ounce steps.

What does My Bar do?

Tick the bottles and mixers you have. The app counts the drinks you can make, and Can make on the Cocktails screen lists only those.

Where do the recipes come from?

Specs follow the International Bartenders Association (IBA) where an official spec exists, and widely published classic specs otherwise. Layer order uses published specific-gravity charts; shaken and stirred drinks include an approximate amount of melted ice.

Drink responsibly

You must be of legal drinking age to use this app. Please drink responsibly and never drink and drive.
