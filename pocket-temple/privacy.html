ENGLISH

Summary

Pocket Temple collects no personal data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not connect to any server of ours.

Data on your device

Your one-sentence journal entries, meditation minutes, moktak, bell and tea counts, and your weather and sound settings are stored only on your device and are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app removes them.

Time and weather

The courtyard follows your device's clock and date. The weather shown is chosen by the app itself from the date; the app does not use your location or any weather service.

Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.
