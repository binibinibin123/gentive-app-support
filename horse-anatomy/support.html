Hands On Horse walks you through a daily hands-on check on a 3D horse: where to feel for a pulse, how to read gut sounds, gum refill and the legs, and what normal is for a healthy adult horse at rest. It also covers body condition scoring, a weight tape and saddle fit, and keeps a dated record for each of your horses on your phone.

How do I send the readings to my vet?

Tap "For the vet" on the Today screen. It lists the day's readings next to the normal ranges and marks anything outside them. Tap "Copy as text" and paste it into a message or email to your vet.

Can I keep records for more than one horse?

Yes. Open the Stable and tap "Add another horse". Each horse has its own records. To delete a horse and all its records, use "Remove this horse" in the Stable.

Can I use °F, inches and pounds?

Yes. Under Units choose Imperial (°F, in, lb) or Metric (°C, cm, kg). Records are stored once and shown in the units you choose.

Where do the normal ranges come from?

Each range lists its source in the app under Stable > Where the numbers come from: university extension services, the Merck Veterinary Manual and Henneke's original body condition paper. Ranges are for a calm, healthy adult horse; foals, work and hot weather change them.

Is this veterinary advice?

No. Hands On Horse does not diagnose anything and is not a substitute for your veterinarian or farrier. When a reading is outside the normal range it tells you when to call. If your horse seems unwell, call your vet.
