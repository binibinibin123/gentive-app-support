Summary

Hands On Horse collects no personal data. There is no account and no sign-up, the app makes no network connections, and it contains no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

The app keeps one save file on your phone with:

  • your horses' names;
  • a dated record of each check: vital signs, body condition scores, leg findings, weight tape measurements, saddle fit results and your notes;
  • study progress (quiz results, bookmarks, tours) and settings such as units.

None of this is sent to us or anyone else. Removing a horse in the Stable deletes its records, and deleting the app deletes the file. If you use iCloud or computer backups, iOS may include it in your device backup.

The vet summary and the clipboard

The app puts text on the clipboard only when you tap "Copy as text" on the For the vet screen. That text is the day's summary for the selected horse. Where it goes after that is up to you: it leaves the app only when you paste it somewhere.

Purchases

This version has no in-app purchases. Every feature is free.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.
