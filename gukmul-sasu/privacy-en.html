Privacy Policy

Data we collect

Hold the Broth! does not collect or transmit any personal data: no name, email, location or device identifiers. There are no ads, no analytics and no purchases.

Data on your device

Stage records and settings (sound, vibration, controls, language) are stored only on your device and never sent anywhere.

Motion sensor

If you turn on tilt controls, the accelerometer is read in real time only to steer the cup; it is not stored or transmitted.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no data from children.
